Our Process

From Idea to Delivery

A structured, transparent workflow so you always know what stage your order is at.

01

Submit Your Requirement

Tell us what you want to produce — product type, target quantity, and any reference designs. Use our quote request form to get started.

02

Consultation & Product Selection

We walk through fabric, fit, customization and packaging options together, and confirm a specification that matches your budget and brand.

03

Design & Specification Review

Our team helps refine sketches, measurements and tech packs so the final spec is production-accurate before sampling begins.

04

Sampling & Prototype

A physical sample is produced so you can evaluate fit, fabric and finish before committing to a full production run.

05

Approval

Once you sign off on the sample, we lock the specification and schedule your order into production.

06

Production

Cutting, sewing and finishing take place according to the approved spec, with progress tracked against your timeline.

07

Quality Inspection

Every batch is checked against the approved sample for fabric, stitching, sizing and finish before it moves to packing.

08

Packaging

Garments are folded, tagged and packed according to your private-label branding and packaging specification.

09

Delivery

Your order is shipped with the documentation needed for a smooth delivery, wherever your business is based.
