Manufacturing Capabilities

Services built for private-label production

From first sketch to shipped order, AAZLI Industries supports every stage of getting your apparel to market.

Service 01

Private Label Manufacturing

Full branding support across woven labels, printed tags, care labels and retail-ready packaging — so every product leaves our facility looking like it belongs to your brand, not ours.

  • ✓Custom woven and printed labels
  • ✓Branded hang tags and packaging
  • ✓Consistent branding across reorders
Service 02

Low MOQ Production

Test new styles and enter the market without committing to bulk-order inventory risk. Our minimums are structured for startups and small labels first.

Starting MOQ: 100 pieces per style

Service 03

Cut & Sew Manufacturing

Every garment is built from your pattern and specification — not a modified blank — giving your brand a genuinely original silhouette and fit.

  • ✓Pattern-making and grading
  • ✓Tech pack development support
  • ✓Custom fit adjustments
Service 04

Design & Sampling Support

We help translate your concept into a production-ready spec — sketches, measurements and a physical sample before any bulk order begins.

  • ✓Tech pack review and adjustment
  • ✓Physical prototype before bulk production
  • ✓Fit and fabric evaluation rounds
Service 05

Materials & Fabric Sourcing

We source fabric matched to your product's function and price point — from everyday cotton basics to performance and fleece fabrications.

Available fabric families: cotton, cotton-poly blends, fleece and performance knits

Global Shipping & Reorder Support

We coordinate shipping documentation and logistics so approved styles can move from first order to reorder with minimal friction.

Shipping regions: worldwide via air and sea freight, FOB or DDP terms available
